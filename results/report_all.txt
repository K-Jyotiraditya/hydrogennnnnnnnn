Attribution of the global enthalpy-balance residual
  The finite-volume radial operator is exactly conservative, so with
  both optional transport terms switched off the balance must close
  to integrator tolerance.  The residual of the full model is the
  enthalpy carried by radial species dispersion, which is omitted on
  purpose: the same eddy transport is already represented in k_er,
  and counting it twice would overstate radial heat transfer.

  VERDICT: Radial resolution changes the duty predictions negligibly (<0.5 conversion points, <10 K on peak wall temperature). 2D is needed for the gradient itself and for radially resolved diagnostics such as coking, not for duty.

Effective radial conductivity sweep
  Published k_er correlations for comparable geometries disagree by a
  factor approaching two; the radial gradient scales roughly as 1/k_er.

  k_er [W/m/K]    peak wall T [K]   peak radial dT [K]    X_CH4 [%]

  Peak radial dT spans 86.5 to 125.6 K over this range.  The
